🚩 Red flag: A "guide" who cannot show a TAAN or NTIA licence card, refuses to put details in writing, or quotes far below market rates.

SOLO & FEMALE TRAVELERS

Women's safety

📞

National women's helpline

1145

Free, confidential, 24/7. Also the One-Stop Crisis Management Centres at major hospitals.

  • Use app taxis (InDrive, Pathao) at night instead of flagging street cabs.
  • Keep the front desk informed of your daily itinerary.
  • Share your live location with someone at home.
  • On treks, hike with a buddy or hire a porter; avoid solo sections after dark.
  • Trust your instinct — leave any situation that feels off and go to a busy place.
